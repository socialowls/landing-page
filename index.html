Case study · The Clinic

Engagement beat awareness.

On LinkedIn, for M Group Holding.

A medical manufacturer with operations in Egypt, Sudan and Ghana. The question: which LinkedIn spend was actually working?

The brief

Make sense of M Group’s LinkedIn: organic posts and paid campaigns, side by side, with one clear answer on where the budget should go.

What we did

Built an interactive performance dashboard covering organic and paid metrics, then compared campaign objectives head to head.

What we found

The engagement-focused campaign delivered strong CTR and follower growth against a brand-awareness boost. Video beat text posts on engagement.

Case study · Branding

The grammar of a brand.

her’, premium sportswear for women.

Branding and brand guidelines for a premium women’s sportswear label, built around one small, possessive mark.

The brief

A sportswear brand for women that feels personal, premium and theirs, not another logo on a waistband.

What we did

The her’ identity and guidelines: the possessive apostrophe, a nude, sage, khaki and gold palette, and type with character.

How we told it

“The Grammar of a Brand”: the thinking behind the identity, turned into a 7-slide carousel and a reel.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What does Social Owls do?

Social Owls is a strategy-first social media and branding agency. We build brand positioning, visual identities and social media content systems that connect content to business results, from strategy and design to community management, paid media and reporting.

Where is Social Owls based?

We have two offices, in Egypt and the UAE, and we work with brands across the UAE & Egypt.

Which industries do you work with?

We have worked with 66 brand partners across travel & tourism, food & beverage, retail, fashion & beauty, tech, medical & aesthetics, engineering & building materials, and events & entertainment.

What makes your approach different?

Most brands don’t have a content problem, they have a strategy problem. We start with positioning and audience behaviour in each market, then build content that supports awareness, intent and conversion, rather than chasing vanity metrics.

Do you create brand identities as well as social media?

Yes. Our branding work covers brand strategy, logo and visual systems, brand guidelines and marketing templates. You can see identities we built on our branding page.
